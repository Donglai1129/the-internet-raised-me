# The internet raised me

The feed is a place to wander rather than a route assigned by one click. Each section pairs two connected references with two discoveries from the broader collection. Small, bounded click effects coexist with chance and recurrence. Related-photo thumbnails let visitors make their own connections.

Every ten sections a brief, skippable question offers a temporary change in the mix. It affects only three sections. The website shows how choice, repetition and a content environment interact without claiming that one image defines a person.

The reveal describes what actually appeared, repeated and was opened. Its most repeated image comes from recorded exposure rather than an initial seed anchor. The optional alternate feed is a labelled simulation. Neither the data nor the final collage diagnoses identity or proves psychological change.
